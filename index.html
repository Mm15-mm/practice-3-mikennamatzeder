Piet Mondrian

Piet Mondrian was a pioneering Dutch painter who helped shape modern abstract art through his use of straight lines, right angles, and primary colors.

Piet Mondrian's paintings are famous for their grid patterns of black lines, white backgrounds, and primary colors (red, blue, and yellow).

1872   Born in Amerserfoort, the Netherlands

1911   Moves to Paris and sees Cubism

1917   Co-founds De Stijl with Theo van Doesburg

1930   Paints Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

1944   Dies in New York
